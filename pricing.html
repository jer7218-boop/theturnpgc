Assessments

Every program at The Turn starts with a baseline. We measure how you move before we change how you swing or train.

Golf Body-Swing Assessment

$225
Required before any lessons

A full screen connecting how your body moves to how your swing works, so your coaching plan is built on your physical reality rather than a generic model.

Fitness Assessment

$175
Required before any fitness sessions

A movement and strength baseline that sets the starting point for your training program and gives us something to measure progress against.

Your assessment comes back to you

Assessment fees are credited

What you pay for your Golf Body-Swing Assessment or Fitness Assessment is credited toward either of the following:

  • A Tier 2 or Tier 1 monthly membership
  • Any package of 8 or more tokens or training sessions
Conditions
  • One assessment credit per customer.
  • Credit may be applied once, to a single purchase.
  • The qualifying package or membership must be purchased at the time of your assessment.
  • Does not apply to junior lessons.
