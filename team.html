Head Performance Coaches

Leading the performance floor

Angus Lee

Co-Founder · Head Performance Coach

Angus brings over 16 years of experience in golf-specific training. He fell for the game at six, watching his father practice on the range, and competed in golf and basketball through high school — where injuries and a lack of strength showed him how much the body drives the swing. That sparked a passion for strength and conditioning at UC Irvine, where he interned as a physical therapy aide.

Today he builds custom programs blending functional movement, body-swing analysis, and golf-specific drills to develop power, speed, balance, and injury resilience — known for delivering measurable results that help players of every level play stronger, smarter, and longer.

PGA Teaching Professional TPI Golf & Fitness Strength & Conditioning Injury Prevention & Post-Rehab

Elliot Bunyaviroch

Co-Founder · Head Performance Coach

A four-year collegiate golfer and former Captain of the UC San Diego men's golf team, Elliot brings a player's-eye perspective to performance coaching. He co-founded The Turn in Dublin, California, after years spent in golf instruction and community development — from coaching juniors at Lake Merced Golf Club to building programs that bring players together.

He works with members across every level, from first-timers to competitive amateurs, pairing technical instruction with the team-first mindset he led with in college to help players build a more confident, repeatable game.

PGA Associate TPI Certified NCAA D1 Golfer UC San Diego Men's Golf Captain Junior & Adult Instruction

Playing Professional · Golf Instructor

Alex Wanxi Sun

A competitive professional golfer with four years of NCAA Division I experience at Seton Hall University, Alex was a senior captain and earned PING All-Northeast Region and All-Big East honors before turning pro. He now competes on the Asher and Moonlight Tours while coaching at The Turn — bringing an elite player's eye to the fundamentals for golfers at every level, in both English and Mandarin Chinese.

NCAA D1 · Seton Hall PING All-Northeast Region Korn Ferry / Asher / Moonlight Pro Tour
Performance & Mobility Strength Coach

Brandon Woo

Founder of Winning Mentality Fitness, Brandon grew up in an athletic family and competed in baseball, boxing, and golf before moving into coaching. His own experience with injury and chronic pain shapes how he trains people: quality of movement comes first, and strength, speed, and explosiveness get built on top of it. He coaches in person and online, works with clients who have stayed with him six years and longer, runs baseball strength and conditioning at every level, and hosts The Winning Mentality Podcast.
